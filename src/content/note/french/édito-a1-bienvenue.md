## R rules

1. In French, the letters "-er" at the end of a word with more than **one** syllable, the "r" become silent, and the "e" changes its sound to "ay". Like "parler".

2. masculine: Le premier -> silent "r" (pruh-mee-ay)
   feminine: La première -> sounded "r" (pruh-mee-air)

3. If the owrd is very short (one syllable), you do pronounce the "r":
   - Mer (sea)
   - Cher (dear)
   - Mars (March)
