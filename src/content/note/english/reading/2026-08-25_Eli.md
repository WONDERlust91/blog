Nov 12, 2016 - Dusting yourself down

## Notes

1. so said sb. 是个倒装 so 代指前面说的话

2. To get rid of any dust that does nevertheless manage to accumulate, the material also has embedded within it a yarn made of conducting carbon-nantube fibres.

   nevertheless 转折的是 To get rid of any dust

3. But as is traditional for technology developed for use in space, Boeing is keen on terrestrial applications, too.

   as 引导的状语从句，翻译为“正如”时，as既有连词的意思，同时还有代词的意思，可能代宾语或主语。这句话中是代主语。真正指代的是后一句话 Boeing is keen on terrestrial applications。即 (Boeing is keen on terrestrial applications) is traditional for technology developed for use in space.
